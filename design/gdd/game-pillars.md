# Pigeon Maxxing — Game Pillars

> **Status**: Locked for pre-production v0.1
> **Last Updated**: 2026-08-27

## Pillar 1 — Every Upgrade Shows

**Definition:** Progression should be physically or audiovisually legible, not confined to larger numbers.

**Design test:** If an upgrade is important enough to change how the player values their build, prefer a visible pigeon/environment/VFX/animation consequence over a stat-only reward.

Implications:
- branch milestones swap or add visual layers;
- total-level thresholds resize/recompose the scene;
- passive income gains helpers/props;
- active-power gains stronger hit animation/VFX;
- milestone feedback is more important than unique art for every numeric level.

## Pillar 2 — One More Growth Stage

**Definition:** The player should nearly always see a concrete, reachable next threshold that promises a meaningful transformation.

**Design test:** If a progression segment has no visible goal within the player's near-term horizon, add/reposition a milestone rather than filling the gap with more grind.

Implications:
- show `Total Upgrade Level / Next Growth` prominently;
- early milestones arrive quickly;
- later milestones expand in spacing but increase spectacle/reward;
- growth thresholds unlock mechanics often enough to refresh the loop.

## Pillar 3 — Tapping Must Feel Good

**Definition:** The game may be an incremental game, but active tapping must have tactile rhythm, combo tension and escalating audiovisual juice.

**Design test:** If holding/watching produces the same moment-to-moment sensation as actively tapping, improve active feedback or active-play mechanics.

Implications:
- responsive peck animation;
- squash/stretch and particles;
- combo system;
- critical hits;
- escalating sound layers;
- restrained screen shake at higher combo tiers.

## Pillar 4 — Absurdity Escalates From Reality

**Definition:** The joke starts with a believable ordinary pigeon and becomes increasingly impossible as a consequence of progression.

**Design test:** If a visual gag could appear at minute one with no loss of meaning, it probably does not belong as a late-game reward.

Implications:
- park/bench/street first;
- human-scale absurdity second;
- traffic/building/city-scale escalation later;
- cosmic/surreal material is earned late;
- visual transformations should tell the story of power accumulation.

## Pillar 5 — Monetization Respects the Loop

**Definition:** Advertising monetizes acceleration and natural breaks, not frustration or accidental interruption.

**Design test:** If an ad would surprise the player during active input, delay it to a result/transition state or make it an explicit rewarded choice.

Implications:
- no fullscreen ads during active tapping or minigame targeting;
- rewarded ads clearly state the reward before playback;
- the baseline game remains viable without rewarded ads;
- portal-specific ad pacing is handled by the platform adapter.

# Anti-Pillars

## No Spreadsheet With a Pigeon Skin

We will not build dozens of stat-only upgrades with no presentation consequence. Large level counts are acceptable only when milestones keep progression visually legible.

## No Random Meme Scrapbook

We will not bolt unrelated viral characters/jokes onto the game merely because they trend. The pigeon world must develop its own coherent visual identity and original IP.

## No Ad Ambushes

We will not trigger ads in the middle of active tapping, aiming, or other time-sensitive interaction.

## No Heavy 3D Scope

We will not turn this into a large 3D world, physics sandbox, open-world game, or content-heavy adventure. The core value is progression, presentation and replayability in a lightweight browser product.

## No Feature Pile Before Core Pacing Works

We will not hide weak clicker progression under excessive modes, currencies, social systems or content. The first 60–90 minutes of the upgrade/growth economy must work before major expansion.
